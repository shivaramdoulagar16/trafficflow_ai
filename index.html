Platform Overview

About FlowSync

FlowSync is a smart city traffic optimization framework designed to replace rigid timing plans with adaptive signal decisions that respond to what streets actually need, second by second.

System architecture

A layered model collects live roadway signals, interprets them with a decision engine, and applies optimized timing plans across intersection clusters.

Smart city value

Faster journeys, safer pedestrian crossings, reliable emergency access, and better public transport progression.

Sustainability impact

Reduced idling lowers fuel waste and emissions while improving the efficiency of existing road infrastructure.

Framework Layers

Layer 01
Sensing

Cameras, loops, GPS traces, pedestrian calls, and public transport feeds.

Layer 02
Fusion

Signal cleaning, conflict resolution, and confidence scoring across sources.

Layer 03
Optimization

Dynamic phase split tuning, offset alignment, and emergency preemption planning.

Layer 04
Operator Interface

Monitoring dashboards, manual overrides, KPI review, and scenario simulation.

Why it matters

As cities grow, the cost of congestion rises across productivity, energy, emergency response, and quality of life. FlowSync helps cities use intelligence—not just more asphalt—to move people more efficiently and sustainably.
